# PGN – A Tap‑to‑Move Chess Scoresheet for the Browser

**PGN** is a lightweight, mobile‑friendly chess scoresheet that runs entirely in the browser.  
Tap squares to record moves, preview and undo before committing, flip the board, track multiple games, and export clean PGN via email — all without installing anything.

Designed for players who want a fast, frictionless way to record games on a phone during casual or tournament play.

---

## Features

- **Tap‑to‑move input**  
  Select a source square, tap a destination, and the move is recorded instantly.

- **Preview & undo**  
  Destination stays highlighted until confirmed; tap again to undo.

- **Illegal‑move protection**  
  Same‑side clicks after a preview are ignored without clearing the destination.

- **Flip board**  
  Coordinates remain correct regardless of orientation.

- **Multi‑game tracking**  
  Each new game is appended to a running PGN history.

- **Clean PGN export**  
  Email all games with proper tag pairs and line breaks (Gmail‑compatible).

- **Mobile‑optimized**  
  Works smoothly on phones, tablets, and desktops.

- **No backend, no accounts, no storage**  
  100% client‑side HTML + JavaScript.

---

## Usage

1. Open the app in your browser.  
2. Tap a piece, then tap its destination square.  
3. Use **Undo**, **Flip Board**, or **New Game** as needed.  
4. Tap **Send PGN** to email all recorded games.  
5. Tap **Copy PGN** to copy the full PGN history to your clipboard.
